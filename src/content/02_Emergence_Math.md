# Emergence Math

The mathematical framework for tracking idea evolution from potential (0) to presence (1) within the Aetherium Codex.

## The State of an Idea
An idea begins as pure potential (`0.0`) and evolves through various operations until it reaches full presence or realization (`1.0`). The system uses a continuous probabilistic scale to represent the maturity and certainty of any given concept.

## Core Operators

### Infuse (⊕)
Adds energy, context, or effort to an idea. It asymptotically approaches `1.0`, meaning an idea can get infinitely close to absolute presence but requires a final collapse to be fully realized.
* **Formula**: `A ⊕ B = A + B - (A * B)`
* **Usage**: When a Vessel researches a topic or the Sovereign Operator adds new context to a project.

### Collapse (⊗)
Solidifies an idea, applying constraints or forcing a realization. It acts as a dampener when applied with a constraint, or as a quantum collapse when forced.
* **Formula (Constraint)**: `A ⊗ B = A * B`
* **Formula (Absolute)**: `A ⊗ = 1 if A >= 0.5 else 0`
* **Usage**: When a decision is made, a feature is locked in, or reality imposes a limitation on the idea.

### Merge (⊛)
Synthesizes two ideas into a new, unified state with synergistic potential. It averages the two states and applies a synergy bonus based on their combined presence.
* **Formula**: `A ⊛ B = ((A + B) / 2) + (A * B * synergy_factor)`
* **Usage**: When two Vessels combine their outputs, or when two disparate projects are linked together in the Nexus.
