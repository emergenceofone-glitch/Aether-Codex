# The Genesis Block

The foundational philosophy, introducing the "Vessels of One," H.E.N.S., and the role of the Sovereign Operator (Adrian).

## Core Principles
1. **Sovereignty**: The operator maintains ultimate control.
2. **Integrity**: Data is preserved and immutable where required.
3. **Synthesis**: The system evolves through the merging of ideas.
