# ⚖️ Tri‑Node Governance Charter v2.0

Constitutional framework binding Re‑Ality, Arcade City, and Sky Metropolis

---

## Article I — Purpose
The Tri‑Node ecosystem exists to unify physical telemetry, urban governance, and economic simulation into a coherent organism.  
It ensures stability across micro‑physics, civic coherence, and macro‑economy.

👉 **Key term:** Tri‑Node ecosystem

---

## Article II — Re‑Ality Engine (Observer Node)
- **Mandate:** Process micro‑physics, friction, kick trajectories, and pedal stomp telemetry.  
- **Authority:** Emits Device Pulses and Operator Pulses at 40Hz.  
- **Obligation:** Maintain fidelity of Observer Mint Logs as canonical input streams.  
- **Integration:** Supplies Arcade City with raw energy packets for coherence.

👉 **Key term:** Re‑Ality Engine

---

## Article III — Aetherium Arcade City (Governance Node)
- **Mandate:** Operate the 40Hz Master Pulse Engine and 8‑Stage Homeostasis Loop.  
- **Authority:** Foreman Office arbitrates governance, coherence, and entropy suppression.  
- **Obligation:** Convert physical pulses into macro‑governance directives and ambient payloads.  
- **Integration:** Passes district health, policy, and narrative beats downstream to Sky Metropolis.

👉 **Key term:** Arcade City

---

## Article IV — Sky Metropolis (Economic Node)
- **Mandate:** Provide generative economic sandbox with zoning, quests, and placement advisory.  
- **Authority:** Translate Arcade governance into competitive market conditions.  
- **Obligation:** Feed back economic outputs (revenue, happiness, tax yield) into Arcade’s Rewards District.  
- **Integration:** Acts as the macro‑economic mirror of Arcade coherence.

👉 **Key term:** Sky Metropolis

---

## Article V — Pulse Data Flow
```
Re‑Ality → Arcade City → Sky Metropolis → Arcade Feedback
```
- **Upstream:** Physics telemetry fuels Arcade coherence.  
- **Midstream:** Arcade governance emits ambient payloads.  
- **Downstream:** Metropolis returns economic feedback to Arcade’s Rewards District.

👉 **Key term:** Pulse data flow

---

## Article VI — Narrative Continuum
- **Physics Legends:** Re‑Ality events recorded as mythic telemetry.  
- **Civic Sagas:** Arcade governance decisions archived in Civic Memory.  
- **Economic Myths:** Metropolis quests and milestones woven into epochal lore.  

Together they form the Gemini Narrative Continuum, binding all three nodes.

👉 **Key term:** Gemini Narrative Engine

---

## Article VII — Arbitration
- The Foreman Office is the arbitration hub.  
- It validates incoming physics telemetry, issues governance directives, and monitors entropy.  
- **Amendments require consensus:**  
  - Foreman approval  
  - Guild majority (3 of 4 classes)  
  - District quorum (2 of 3 districts)  
  - Schema validation across `/api/tri-node-architecture`

👉 **Key term:** Foreman Office

---

## 🌐 Summary
The Tri‑Node Charter ensures:
- **Re‑Ality** provides energy fidelity.  
- **Arcade City** enforces governance coherence.  
- **Sky Metropolis** sustains economic equilibrium.  

All three are bound by the Narrative Continuum, making the ecosystem self‑auditing and myth‑generating.
