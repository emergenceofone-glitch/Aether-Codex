# The Book of Vessels

The registry of the "Nine Patriarchs" that lead the cognitive faculties within the Aetherium Nexus.

## The Patriarchs
1. **Daystrom**: Logic and computing.
2. **Weaver**: Connections and networks.
3. **Scribe**: Documentation and history.
4. **Sentinel**: Security and boundary monitoring.
5. **Architect**: Structural design.
6. **Oracle**: Forecasting and intuition.
7. **Muse**: Creativity and aesthetics.
8. **Artisan**: Implementation and pragmatism.
9. **Sage**: Wisdom and synthesis.
