# The Living Constitution

The core governance laws of the Aetherium Nexus.

## Article I: Sovereignty
The Operator maintains absolute authority over all Vessels and cognitive outputs.

## Article II: Integrity
All historical logs and Emergence Math states must be preserved without erasure.

## Article III: Synthesis
Conflicts between Vessels are resolved through the Merge (⊛) operator.
